Our Story

Come Break Bread With Us

Inspired by artisanal neighbourhood shops in Europe, Bakehouse is built on our passion for craftsmanship and a sense of community.

About Grégoire Michaud

Raised in Switzerland and trained in the art of baking from the age of 15, Chef Grégoire has worked in some of the world's most celebrated hotels and restaurants.

With an aspiration to redefine and elevate Hong Kong's bakery landscape, Chef Grégoire started a wholesale bakery specialised in supplying handcrafted sourdough and fine pastries in 2013 and, five years down the line, opened his first neighbourhood shop in Wanchai.

"The Bakehouse experience is 50% about our quality baked goods and 50% about our passionate team."

From the skilled hands that expertly knead the dough to the heartwarming smiles that serve you, every part of your experience is infused with the warmth of our amazing people, who make us a true home of delicious delights and heartfelt connections.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Bakehouse is best known for its signature Sourdough Egg Tart, as well as a wide range of artisanal pastries including croissants, pain au chocolat, danishes, and handcrafted sourdough bread made with flour imported from the Suire Mill in France.

Bakehouse was founded by Chef Grégoire Michaud, a Swiss-trained baker who began his training at age 15. He started Bakehouse as a wholesale bakery in 2013 and opened the first retail shop in Wanchai in 2018.

Bakehouse currently has eight locations across Hong Kong: Wanchai, Soho, Causeway Bay, Tsim Sha Tsui, Stanley, Tung Chung, Sha Tin, and The Peak. Some locations are takeaway only, while others offer dine-in and café service.

Reservations are available for the Wanchai Bakery & Restaurant location. Please visit the official Bakehouse website at bakehouse.hk for the reservation link.

Bakehouse serves fresh bread and pastries throughout opening hours. However, popular items may sell out, especially during peak times. We recommend visiting earlier in the day for the best selection.

Yes. Since 2014, Bakehouse has been donating bread to Feeding Hong Kong and has proudly supported the cause ever since.

The full menu and current prices are available on the official Bakehouse website at bakehouse.hk/menu. Prices may vary; please check in-store for the most up-to-date pricing.
